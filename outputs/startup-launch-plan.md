# Laven AI - Startup Launch Plan

Status: Director planning. No Executor assigned; no implementation, accounts, paid calls, purchases, or deployment performed.

## Product Outcome

Deliver a real online AI-companion product that eligible users can access outside the developers' computers. The release must use actual authentication, cloud persistence, protected AI integration, and live voice interaction. Local prototypes and mock data can support development/testing but are not evidence of a completed online MVP.

The first release remains the selected owner/invitee private trial. A public paid release is a subsequent business decision, not automatically authorized by the startup clarification.

## Confirmed Platform

The owner reconfirms an online production web app accessed through mobile and desktop browsers. Initial validation targets remain Android Chrome and desktop Chrome. Retain Next.js/TypeScript, Supabase, Cloudflare, and the cascaded OpenRouter pipeline.

The deployable online product is the outcome. A browser-based delivery platform does not make this a local-only app. Native Android/Play Store and native iOS delivery are outside this release's selected scope.

## Four-Person Team

The owner reports a four-person project team with limited experience and no clear work ownership yet. The previously suggested product/frontend/backend/voice division is a proposal, not an approved assignment or equity arrangement.

The Director defines milestones, writes bounded work briefs, reviews evidence, and recommends tradeoffs. The team must identify one responsible person and one reviewer for each actual assignment. Human responsibilities, Executor identities, and availability remain unassigned.

Milestone numbering defines gates, not a fixed calendar. Where authentication is needed to test real voice safely, implement the minimum protected account path alongside voice feasibility. Do not delay model feasibility until after building every screen.

## Release Acceptance

- An eligible user can access the deployed product on the selected platform without a developer's local server.
- Authentication and allowlist eligibility are real and enforced at protected APIs and database access boundaries.
- Voice replies use actual validated provider routes; simulated listening/speaking or sample responses do not count as working AI.
- Thai/English quality, speed support, interruption, captions, and latency are tested and reported honestly. The three-second target retains its original measurement, including utterance-end detection.
- Account data is isolated; memory off, deleted-fact exclusions, history expiry, account deletion, and late-job guards behave as selected.
- Daily/monthly limits are enforced and provider charges measured. The configured USD 10 ceiling is not claimed to guarantee sufficient funding or zero billing overshoot.
- Provider credentials remain server-side. Text/audio retention behavior and unverified provider retention are clearly documented.
- A deployment/recovery procedure exists, failures are visible to the team, and real-user rollout has a concrete review gate.

Sentence-level captions remain permitted temporarily for a clearly identified prototype under Step 182; the final selected word-level caption requirement has not been removed from the product.

## Budget Discipline

Prefer the selected free infrastructure tiers where actual capability and limits permit them, but verify suitability before committing. Real voice inference, domain registration, email delivery, or infrastructure upgrades may require funding; do not describe the startup as guaranteed free to operate.

Do not add a native client, paid monitoring service, embedding provider, in-app feedback integration, or replacement AI route merely as a routine implementation choice. Prepare the cost/scope impact for Director/owner review first.
